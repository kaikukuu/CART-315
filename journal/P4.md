2026-11-15
I decided that the ideas I had previously mentioned were way out of the scope of my current abilities/timeline. During this session I decided to implement my first change; that being the vein-like structures that connect back to the red star to give a more alien-like vibe to the environment. My hope was to use the biological appearance and qualities to make the scene seem alive with unnerving implications. Initially I was not sure how to approach making something like a curving cylinder but it turned out to be a lot simpler than I thought (but still difficult to get it to look right). To create these structures I learned about splines again and used the spline editor along with the spline extrude component to make big tubes. It's not an incredibly complex change but it does push the environment closer towards my goal and aligns better with the visuals than the stairs did. I still do like the idea of having a structure the player can get up on to escape the blackhole but I feel that for the sake of this project I really need to focus my idea. 

This meant that I decided, for now, to forget about making the player able to climb up onto the tubes and take away the moving platform. Both of these were for me to test what might be the best way to give the player an escape but of course it's not as easy as that. I liked the floating platform (the ones that don't move) and crossings concept but I can't get it to fit into what I'm picturing. I still have to properly implement another player interaction mechanic and I've been really stuck on what to do after "killing my darlings" (aka the ideas I couldn't get to). So I think I'll probably go with making the blackhole have a gravitation effect or the other, maybe more ambitous, make the connection between the player collecting stars and the brightness of the red star? I'm trying really hard not  to get too invested and make myself more work...
